Privacy & data

Plain-language notice of what this app stores and where.

  • What: your habits, check-ins, scorecard entries and weekly reviews. Nothing else.
  • Where: always on this device, in the app's local storage. Only if you sign in for sync is a copy also stored in your own account (MongoDB) - sign out removes the session, and the local copy stays yours.
  • Who can see it: only you - the cloud copy lives behind your account credentials.
  • Tracking: none. No analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers, no marketing email.
  • Your rights: export a full copy any time (Backup above), delete habits you no longer want, or clear everything by uninstalling the app.
